# How I use AI when starting a new project

## LinkedIn Summary

When I take an initial call about a new project, I put the conversation details into my daily note and use that to start the job record.

My automation creates the job using the client's name and address, adds it to my job queue, and updates the index. If there's an address, it also adds a Google Maps image of the property. I'll usually do some preliminary research based on what the client and I discussed before the first visit.

After the site visit, I dictate a recap to the AI, often hands free on the way home or to the next project. I'm recounting the conversation and what I observed, rather than recording the meeting word for word.

By the time I reach my next destination, the job record is in place, the commentary has been added, and research into codes, materials, and estimating can already be underway.

For a straightforward project, I may have an estimate sent out by then. If it needs more investigation, I pick that up at my next available opportunity with the notes and initial research already organized.

## Overview

My intake process starts with the first conversation about a project. I capture the details in my daily note, use automation to establish the job record, and begin preliminary research before the first site visit.

After the visit, I add a dictated recap so the information from the conversation and the property can become part of the same record. Research and document preparation can then move forward while I'm away from the laptop.

## Role

I speak with the client, visit the property, and provide the context for the work. I dictate what was discussed and what I observed, then direct the research and estimating that follow.

The automation handles creating and indexing the job record, adding available location imagery, and organizing the information I provide.

## Scope Of Work

The workflow covers the initial call, job creation, preliminary research, the first site visit, the visit recap, and the next steps toward an estimate.

It connects daily notes with the job record so the initial conversation and later commentary are available together.

## Key Actions

### Capture the initial conversation

I put the initial call details into my daily note. The job automation uses the client's name and address to create the record, add it to my job queue, and update the index.

### Add property context and prepare for the visit

When an address is available, the workflow adds a Google Maps image of the property to the job. I also use the initial conversation to guide preliminary research before meeting the client on site.

### Dictate the site visit recap

After the visit, I repeat as much of the relevant information as I can to the AI. This is my account of the discussion and observations, not a verbatim transcript. I often dictate it hands free while traveling home or to the next project.

### Continue the research and documentation

The recap adds detail to the job record and gives the AI context for research into applicable codes, materials, and estimating. By my next destination, the record can be updated and that work underway.

## Important Features

Each stage builds on the information already captured. The initial call starts the record, the property image adds location context, and the site visit recap adds the details needed to develop the work further.

Automatic indexing also keeps the new job connected to my existing queue of work.

## Constraints And Decisions

The next step depends on the complexity of the project. A straightforward scope may be ready for an estimate quickly. A more involved project needs additional research, which I continue when I next have time available.

## Deliverables

An indexed job record with initial call details, property imagery when an address is available, site visit commentary, and supporting research. For simpler projects, the workflow may also produce an estimate ready to send to the client.

## Outcome

I can leave a site visit and continue developing the job through voice input. By the time I arrive at my next destination, the information can be organized and the next steps already moving. In straightforward cases, I may have an estimate out the door.

For more complex work, I return to a record that already contains the visit recap and initial research.

## Tools And Methods

AI assisted intake, daily notes, automated job creation and indexing, Google Maps property imagery, hands free dictation, project research, and estimate preparation.

## Evidence And Limitations

This describes my established workflow and the pace I have experienced. The amount of work completed between destinations depends on the project. No client identities, addresses, financial figures, or private project details are included.
