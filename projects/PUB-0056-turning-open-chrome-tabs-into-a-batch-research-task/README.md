# Turning Open Chrome Tabs into a Batch Research Task

## LinkedIn Summary

I sometimes end up with 30 or more tabs open in Chrome on my Android phone. Pages I want to research, things I want to save, or something I want to come back to.

I used to copy each link individually and paste it into Codex so I could research it and add it to my knowledge base.

I recently found that I can select the tabs together and copy their links as a batch. Then I can send the whole list to ChatGPT or Codex as one research task.

That fits into the research and knowledge base skills I already have. The links are the starting point, and those skills handle reviewing the material and adding the useful information to my knowledge system.

It's saved me a lot of time copying and pasting links one page at a time on my phone.

## Overview

My phone often has a collection of open pages that I want to research or save. Previously, getting those pages into my knowledge workflow meant opening each tab, copying its link, and pasting it into Codex.

I found that I can select tabs together in Chrome on my Android device and copy their links as a batch. That changes the handoff from a series of individual links to one list.

## Role

I select the material I want to work through and send the list to ChatGPT or Codex. My existing research and knowledge capture skills provide the instructions for reviewing the material and saving useful findings.

## Scope Of Work

A batch can contain 30 or more open tabs. The workflow starts with the links already collected through my browsing and carries them into a research task.

Copying the links is the intake step. The pages still need to be read and assessed before their contents become knowledge records.

## Key Actions

- Select the open Chrome tabs I want to include.
- Copy their links together.
- Send the list to ChatGPT or Codex with the research request.
- Use my existing skills to review the sources and capture useful information.
- Add the resulting material to the appropriate place in my knowledge system.

## Important Features

This fits the research process I already have. I changed how the source list gets into the task, rather than building a separate knowledge system for mobile browsing.

My knowledge capture instructions also call for checking existing notes before creating new ones. Related findings can extend an existing record instead of producing another overlapping note.

## Constraints And Decisions

A copied URL is a reference to a page, not a copy of its contents. If a source cannot be accessed, that needs to remain visible as a gap in the research.

The batch also needs enough direction to explain what I want from the material. Saving a useful finding and keeping every page verbatim are different requests.

## Deliverables

The initial handoff is a batch of source links. The intended output is researched information saved in the relevant knowledge records, with useful source references retained.

## Outcome

This has saved me a lot of the manual work of copying and pasting each page on my phone. It makes it easier to hand over a collection of tabs while continuing to use the research and knowledge capture process I already have.

## Tools And Methods

Chrome on Android, batch link capture, ChatGPT or Codex, and existing research and knowledge capture skills.

## Evidence And Limitations

This describes the workflow I found on my own device. It is not a version-specific Chrome tutorial or a claim that every linked page can be accessed automatically. The time saving is my assessment, not a measured benchmark.
