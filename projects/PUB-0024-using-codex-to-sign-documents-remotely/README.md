## Overview

This record documents a supervised document workflow in which Codex helps Daniel prepare a signed copy while he is away from his desk. The source may not be a native electronic-signature form. It may instead be a raw picture or scan of a physical document that has already been signed. The work includes placing a signature and date in the intended fields, checking the completed image, regenerating the copy from the original source when a placement needs correction, and preparing the verified result for delivery.

The remote aspect is the practical point. Daniel can direct the task from elsewhere instead of waiting to return to the computer where the source document is stored. The workflow is still review-driven: Codex assists with document handling, while Daniel decides what should be signed and verifies the final copy.

## Role

Daniel selects the document, confirms that he is authorized to sign it, directs the remote session, decides where the signature and date belong, and reviews the completed copy. Codex assists with the repetitive image and document-handling work under that direction.

## Scope Of Work

The scope is limited to preparing a document image for review and delivery. It includes identifying the intended signature and date fields, placing the requested markings on a raw picture or scan when needed, preserving existing writing and printed labels, checking the result, regenerating from the original source when necessary, and preparing the accepted copy for delivery.

It does not include deciding whether a document should be signed, replacing legal advice, performing notarization, verifying identity, or releasing a document without human review.

## Key Actions

The workflow begins with a plain-language instruction describing the document and the intended signature task. When the source is a raw picture or scan of a physical signed page, Codex helps inspect the relevant area and place the signature and date without covering existing content. Daniel then checks the result, including a focused review of the signature area. If a placement is wrong, the accepted version is regenerated from the original source rather than layered repeatedly on top of an earlier attempt.

Once the copy passes review, it can be prepared for delivery. The remote version is intended to let Daniel direct these steps while away from his desk.

## Important Features

- Remote direction from away from the primary workstation.
- Support for raw pictures or scans of physical signed documents.
- A practical option when the source is not a native electronic-signature form.
- Cursive-style signature rendering when a typed signature is needed for the reviewed copy.
- Awareness of existing handwriting, printed labels, and occupied fields.
- Regeneration from the original source image for clean corrections.
- Focused visual inspection before delivery.
- Human control over authorization, placement, review, and release.

## Constraints And Decisions

This is supervised document preparation, not an unattended signing service. A conventional electronic-signature document may not need this process at all. The use case described here is the less tidy situation where the source arrives as a raw picture or scan of a physical signed document and there is no clean native form to operate. The public record does not claim legal enforceability, electronic-signature compliance, notarization, identity verification, or a native integration with a signature platform.

Client identity, project details, document contents, signature images, addresses, filenames, and account information remain private. No pricing or dimensions are part of this record.

## Deliverables

The deliverable is a reviewed copy of the source image with the requested signature and date markings placed in the appropriate fields and prepared for delivery. The public record documents the method without reproducing the private document.

## Outcome

Codex can reduce the friction of a document-signing task when Daniel is away from his desk. The value is not handing over signing authority. It is having a remote assistant for careful placement, correction, visual checking, and file preparation while the human remains responsible for the document.

## Tools And Methods

The workflow uses Codex, remote direction, a raw picture or scan of an original physical document, image editing, cursive-style signature rendering, focused visual inspection, regeneration from the original source, and human verification before delivery. It is not presented as a replacement for a conventional electronic-signature platform when a native digital form is available.
