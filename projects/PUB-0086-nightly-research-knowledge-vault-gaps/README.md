# Nightly Research That Fills Gaps in My Knowledge Vault

## LinkedIn Summary

I have a research agent that runs every night and looks through my daily notes and existing notes for context that's missing from my knowledge vault.

That might be a company I've mentioned, a construction assembly method, or a subject that came up during the day but hasn't been researched yet. It checks what I already have, researches the gaps, and connects that information to the relevant notes.

Everything is stored in Markdown in Obsidian. The research can sit alongside the work that prompted it, with links between related topics, companies, and project information.

I can capture what happened during the day without stopping to research every unfamiliar reference. The nightly agent picks up that additional work from the notes I've already made.

## Overview

I use a research agent that runs nightly to review my daily notes and existing knowledge vault for missing context. It looks for references that would benefit from additional research, checks what is already documented, and adds relevant information with connections to the existing notes.

The subjects come from my own work and observations. A company mentioned in a note or a construction assembly method can become a research topic without me having to prepare a separate research request for every reference.

## Role

I capture the work, conversations, and subjects that come up during my day. The agent handles the additional research and organization around those records.

## Scope Of Work

The automation reviews existing Markdown notes, identifies gaps in the surrounding information, researches those subjects, and links the resulting context into the knowledge vault.

Examples include company background, construction assembly methods, and other subjects mentioned in my notes that are not yet adequately documented.

## Key Actions

- Review daily notes and existing knowledge notes each night.
- Check what information is already available in the vault.
- Identify references that need additional research or context.
- Research those gaps and save the findings in Markdown.
- Connect the findings to relevant existing notes.

## Important Features

The existing notes provide the starting point. The agent has context about why a subject came up and can look for related information already in the vault before adding more.

Markdown keeps the information straightforward to work with. Research, company information, construction methods, and project context can be stored as readable notes and connected through links.

Those connections matter because a research note is more useful when I can find it from the work that led to it. A company reference in a daily note can connect to its background information, while an assembly reference can connect to a more detailed explanation of the method.

## Constraints And Decisions

I keep the research connected to the subjects appearing in my own records. The purpose is to add missing context around the work I am doing, with the existing vault checked first.

The additional research also remains distinct from my account of what happened. Information about a construction method provides context; it does not by itself establish that I used that method on a particular job.

## Deliverables

Additional research notes and expanded context in Markdown, connected to relevant daily notes, companies, construction topics, and project information.

## Outcome

I can record something during the day and have the surrounding research handled through the nightly workflow. My knowledge vault develops from the subjects in those notes, with related information linked together for later reference.

## Tools And Methods

Obsidian, Markdown files, a scheduled AI research agent, existing-note review, contextual research, and links between related records.

## Evidence And Limitations

This describes my reported workflow. It does not claim that every missing topic is identified or that the resulting research makes the vault complete.
