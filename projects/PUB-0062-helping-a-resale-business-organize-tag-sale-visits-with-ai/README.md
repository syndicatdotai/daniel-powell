## Overview

I helped a small business that sources items from tag sales for resale use ChatGPT and Codex to organize its search and plan visits.

The work connected finding publicly advertised sales with deciding the order in which to visit them. The aim was to make more of the available time by organizing the stops into a practical route.

## Role

I showed the business how to use the tools for finding listings, organizing the information, and planning the sequence of visits. My contribution was helping them apply AI to that part of their existing business.

## Scope Of Work

The workflow covered publicly advertised tag sales across the area they wanted to visit. It included gathering listings, organizing the potential stops, and using that information to plan a route.

## Key Actions

I demonstrated how ChatGPT and Codex could help identify sales in the target area and bring the listings together for planning.

We worked on putting the stops into a practical order so the search results could support a day of visits. The objective was to fit in more sales while reducing unnecessary travel between locations.

## Important Features

Finding sales and planning the visits were treated as connected tasks. The organized listings provided the basis for deciding where to go and in what order.

That gave the business a method for working from a collection of advertisements toward a usable plan for the day.

## Constraints And Decisions

The route needed to serve the business's goal of visiting as many sales as practical in the available time. The sequence of stops was part of that planning, alongside which sales to include.

## Deliverables

A demonstrated workflow for finding public tag sale listings, organizing them, and arranging visits into a practical route.

## Outcome

The business was shown how to use ChatGPT and Codex for its tag sale planning. The intended benefit was more productive sourcing trips with less backtracking between stops.

## Tools And Methods

ChatGPT, Codex, public sale listings, organized stop information, and route planning.

This describes the assistance I provided. No measured increase in visits or sales is claimed. Business identity, locations, and commercial details are omitted.
