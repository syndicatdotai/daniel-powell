# Adding field photos and context to job records with Codex Remote

## LinkedIn Summary

When I'm at work in the field, I can upload a photo directly into Codex Remote and explain what's going on in the picture.

I tell it which job the photo belongs to and what I want to remember about it. Codex puts the image in the appropriate job folder and updates the job file with that context, so I can find the photo and the explanation together later.

That's easier for me than coming back to the pictures at the end of the day, uploading them somewhere else, and trying to remember what I meant to document. I can take care of it while I'm standing there looking at the work.

## Overview

When I'm working in the field, I can send a photo into Codex Remote with an explanation of what I'm looking at. I identify the job and describe what I want recorded. Codex files the image with that job and adds the context to the job record.

This is how I use it within my existing job documentation workflow.

## Role

I take the photo, identify the job, and supply the explanation. Codex handles placing the image in the appropriate folder and updating the associated job file from that instruction.

## Scope Of Work

The workflow covers photo intake, filing, and written context for future reference. The image and the job note serve different purposes: the photo shows the work, while the note records what I wanted to document about it.

## Key Actions

- Upload the field photo directly into Codex Remote.
- Identify which job it belongs to.
- Explain what the photo shows and what should be remembered.
- Have Codex save the image in the appropriate job folder.
- Have Codex update the job file with the accompanying context.

## Important Features

I provide the image and explanation in the same conversation. The explanation becomes part of the job record instead of depending on my ability to remember the reason for the photo later.

The saved image also stays with the job's other files, so future reference starts from the job record.

## Constraints And Decisions

The filing instruction needs enough context to identify the right job. My explanation supplies details that may not be apparent in the image itself. The workflow uses both the photograph and what I say about it.

## Deliverables

- A field photo stored in the appropriate job folder.
- An updated job file containing the accompanying explanation.
- A record of the observation that can be revisited with the image.

## Outcome

For me, this is easier than setting the pictures aside to upload and organize later. I can handle the filing and explanation while I'm at the work location, rather than reconstructing the context during a separate administrative session.

## Tools And Methods

Codex Remote is the intake point for the photo and my instructions. The remote Codex session works with the existing job folders and written job records. The method combines a photograph, a job reference, and a plain language explanation in one request.

## Evidence And Limitations

This record describes my own workflow. It does not include private job photographs or client details. No measured time saving or independent accuracy test is claimed.
