# Starting site documentation with real-time AI

## LinkedIn Summary

I'm experimenting with real-time AI on construction sites to start the documentation while I'm still looking at the work.

I dictate measurements, job conditions, things that need attention, and my raw thoughts as I go. The AI captures and organizes that information into notes that can become a punch list or an estimate.

It's similar to recording a video with commentary, except the information is already being organized for the paperwork as I capture it.

That gives me a jump start on the documentation during the visit. It starts with the observations on site instead of waiting until I get back to the office.

## Overview

I'm experimenting with real-time AI to begin documenting a construction site visit as I make observations. I speak as I look at the work, describing measurements, conditions, and items that need attention.

The documentation starts with the information being captured on site. Those spoken notes become the basis for punch lists, estimates, and other documents.

## Role

I make the observations and explain what I'm seeing. AI helps capture and organize that commentary so it can be used in the project documentation.

## Scope Of Work

The experiment covers dictation during the visit, organization of field notes, and development of those notes into documents such as punch lists and estimates.

## Key Actions

I talk through the work in ordinary language. That can include a measurement and what it relates to, a condition that affects the job, or a thought about what needs to happen next.

I want to preserve those details without first having to compose a finished note. The next step is using the captured information to assemble the appropriate document.

## Important Features

This resembles recording a video with spoken commentary as a reference. The additional opportunity with AI is to organize the commentary and use it directly in document preparation.

The goal is to leave the visit with more of that work already developed from what I said on site.

## Constraints And Decisions

The output depends on the information I provide. A punch list needs the items that require attention; an estimate needs enough detail about the intended work. The spoken observations provide the context for that next step.

## Deliverables

Captured field notes and, as the experiment develops, punch lists or estimates prepared from the site commentary.

## Outcome

This gives me a jump start on documentation during the visit. Capturing and organizing the observations becomes part of the site work, rather than a task that starts when I return to the office.

## Tools And Methods

AI voice interaction, field dictation, note organization, and document preparation from spoken observations.

## Evidence And Limitations

This is an ongoing experiment. No specific turnaround result is claimed, and no client information, measurement values, or financial figures are included.
