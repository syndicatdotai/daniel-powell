# Using Voice for Almost All of My AI Instructions

## LinkedIn Summary

I'm using voice to text almost exclusively to interact with AI at this point. I rarely use the keyboard to give it instructions anymore.

I dictate what I need done, add the context, and let AI organize it. That includes notes, documents, and updates across my projects.

Even when I'm sitting at my computer with a keyboard in front of me, I'll usually dictate the request.

## Overview

Voice to text has become my usual way of interacting with AI. I use it for almost all of my instructions, including when I'm working at my desktop.

## Role

I dictate the request and provide the context. AI organizes that input and uses it to carry out the work I've described.

## Scope Of Work

My dictated requests include notes, documents, and updates across projects. The same input method works for the field information I need to capture and the instructions I give while at my computer.

## Key Actions

I describe what needs to happen and include the relevant details in the dictation. I can provide the request and its supporting context together.

## Important Features

Voice input fits into the workflows I already use for documentation and project updates. Having a keyboard available rarely changes how I choose to give AI its instructions.

## Constraints And Decisions

I use dictation for almost all of this interaction. That reflects my current working preference and the way I provide information.

## Deliverables

The immediate input is a dictated request with context. Depending on the task, the resulting work can be a note, document, or project update.

## Outcome

Dictation has become my default way of giving AI instructions, both in the field and at my desk.

## Tools And Methods

Voice to text captures my spoken instructions. AI then organizes the information and applies it through the relevant workflow.

## Evidence And Limitations

This describes my personal use of voice input. The frequency is my own observation, and I have not measured it as a percentage of requests.
