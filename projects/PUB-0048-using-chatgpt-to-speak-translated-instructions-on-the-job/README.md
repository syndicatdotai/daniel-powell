# Using ChatGPT to speak translated instructions on the job

## LinkedIn Summary

I use ChatGPT to translate instructions or questions from English when I'm talking with contractors, subs, or other people on the job who speak another language.

I can have it play the translated question out loud for them. I don't have to try to pronounce words in a language I don't speak or hand over my phone for someone to read the translation. I can explain what I need in English and let ChatGPT say it in their language.

## Overview

I use ChatGPT to translate English instructions and questions for contractors, subcontractors, and other people on the job who speak another language. I can then play the translated response aloud for the person I'm talking to.

## Role

I supply the question or instruction and specify the language needed. ChatGPT handles the translation and spoken output.

## Scope Of Work

This is a way to communicate a particular question or instruction during a jobsite conversation. It starts with what I want to say in English and produces wording the other person can listen to in their language.

## Key Actions

- Give ChatGPT the English question or instruction.
- Specify the language for the translation.
- Play the translated response aloud for the person on the job.

## Important Features

The translated words can be heard as well as read. I don't need to attempt the pronunciation myself, and the other person can listen instead of reading the response from my screen.

## Constraints And Decisions

The request needs to identify the intended language and make clear what I want to communicate. Translating the wording and establishing that the other person understood it are separate parts of the conversation.

## Deliverables

- A translated version of the English question or instruction.
- Spoken playback of that translation.

## Outcome

I can communicate the question aloud in another language while supplying my own wording in English. This removes the need for me to read unfamiliar words out loud or rely only on showing translated text.

## Tools And Methods

The workflow combines ChatGPT translation with spoken playback. OpenAI documents Read aloud among the ChatGPT Android app's response actions and explains that ChatGPT Voice can be asked to speak another language. [ChatGPT Android App FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8142208), [ChatGPT Voice](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001274/).

## Evidence And Limitations

This record describes my own use of ChatGPT for jobsite communication. It does not document a particular contractor conversation or a tested level of translation accuracy. The exact playback controls depend on the interface in use.
