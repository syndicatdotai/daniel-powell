# A Memorable Notary Appointment and Situational Awareness

## LinkedIn Summary

About eleven years ago, I received one of the most memorable notary calls I have ever handled.

The caller had a strong European accent and needed an affidavit notarized. He asked me to meet him at a local park. The location and the overall arrangement felt unusual enough that I asked the person who was with me at the time to come along.

They did not seem worried, but I told them I would be more comfortable having someone there. It was a simple precaution, and I am still glad I made that decision.

When we arrived, the signer was driving a custom-painted semi-truck. The truck was covered with symbols that appeared gang-related and large images of weapons painted across the exterior. It was not the setting I expected for a routine notary appointment.

To complete the notarization, I climbed into the cab of the truck with the signer and the paperwork. The person who came with me stayed nearby and recorded the interaction while I handled the documents.

Nothing bad happened. The appointment remained calm, I completed the notarization, and we went our separate ways.

What made the experience memorable was the contrast between the task and the setting. Notarizing an affidavit is normally straightforward. This appointment took place in a park, inside the cab of a heavily customized semi-truck, while someone recorded the meeting from nearby.

It also reinforced the value of paying attention when a situation feels unusual. I did not cancel the appointment or create a confrontation. I took a practical precaution, stayed aware of my surroundings, completed the work, and moved on.

More than a decade later, I still remember that truck and that meeting clearly. Notary work can place you in some unexpected situations, and practical judgment matters just as much as being prepared to complete the document correctly.
