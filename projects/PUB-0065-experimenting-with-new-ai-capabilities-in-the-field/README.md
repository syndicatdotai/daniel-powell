# Experimenting with new AI capabilities in the field

## LinkedIn Summary

When a new AI model or feature comes out, I start thinking about where I could try it in the field.

There are tasks I already use AI for, and others where I can see an opportunity to try something different. A new capability gives me a reason to revisit how I'm doing the work and see what else is possible.

I enjoy that part of it. Being out on a job, seeing something that needs to get done, and thinking, I wonder if I can use this here.

## Overview

New AI models and features give me opportunities to experiment with how I work in the field. I'm interested in where a new capability might fit into an actual task, including uses I haven't tried before.

Construction work provides the context for those questions. The task in front of me is a starting point for thinking about what to try.

## Role

I bring the knowledge of the work and identify opportunities to experiment. I decide what I want to try and consider whether the result fits the task.

## Scope Of Work

This is an ongoing interest in applying new AI capabilities to field work. It includes reconsidering existing workflows and exploring other ways to use the tools as they develop.

## Key Actions

When a model or feature becomes available, I think about where it could fit into my day. That may involve a task I already use AI for or something I would like to approach differently.

The opportunity is to try the capability in the context of the work and see what it can contribute.

## Important Features

Experimenting in the field connects the tool to the circumstances in which I would actually use it. The question becomes whether it helps with the task at hand and fits the way the work is being done.

## Constraints And Decisions

A new release gives me something to explore. Whether I continue using it for a particular task depends on what I find when I try it.

## Deliverables

The focus is exploration of possible uses and adjustments to workflows. This account does not describe a single completed implementation or a specific output.

## Outcome

I find this an interesting part of using AI in construction. New capabilities give me more opportunities to experiment with the work I encounter in the field.

## Tools And Methods

AI models and features, practical experimentation, and the context of ongoing field work.

## Evidence And Limitations

This describes my perspective on experimentation. It makes no claims about a specific model release, measured improvement, or client project.
