# Coordinating an Office-to-Conference-Room Renovation

**Public project ID:** PUB-0089

One of the projects I managed at Infinity Group involved converting existing offices into a conference room. I established the schedule, bought out subcontractors, communicated with the client and contractors, and remained on site through completion.

I used sketches to help communicate the work. Being able to draw a detail or arrangement gave the client and contractors a shared reference during discussions. It was a practical way to explain what needed attention and connect the conversation to the physical space.

My hands-on construction experience also informed how I supervised the work. When contractors needed direction on installing certain materials correctly, I explained the installation methods to them. That practical knowledge allowed me to give specific guidance on how the work should be carried out, alongside my scheduling and coordination responsibilities.

The coordination extended beyond the main room layout. Door hardware and planning for fire-alarm device relocation were among the details associated with the renovation. Those items needed attention alongside the finishes and other subcontracted work.

My responsibility was to organize that work and keep communication moving between the people involved. Establishing a schedule was one part of the job; staying on site through completion meant continuing that coordination as the renovation progressed.

I also handled smaller additional projects on the campus alongside the conference-room conversion. That added another layer of coordination around the main assignment, with separate pieces of work requiring attention.

The project reflects a practical combination of skills I have used throughout my construction work: understanding an existing space, explaining details through sketches, arranging subcontractors, and following the work through to completion. Drawing and field management were closely connected. The sketches supported the conversations, and the on-site coordination carried those conversations into the renovation work.
