# AI-Assisted Research and a Custom Dashboard for a Private Investigator

A private investigator hired me to gather and organize information about an individual on behalf of a client. The assignment combined public-source research with a practical demonstration of how AI tools could support the investigator's work.

My role was research and technical support to the investigator. He reviewed the information and determined what to pass to his client. Responsibility for the investigation and client reporting remained with him.

## Gathering information across public sources

The work used AI-assisted collection and review of public social-media material, online recordings, government documents, and business records. Transcripts made spoken material searchable and provided a way to locate relevant passages across longer recordings.

Public government documents added another source of information about the individual. Business-entity research extended across multiple states to trace entity formation and identify connections relevant to the assignment.

The research brought those materials together for the investigator to examine. Different sources contributed different kinds of information, from an individual's public statements to the names and entities appearing in official records.

## Organizing names, entities, and associations

A lightweight relationship map organized names, associates, and business entities identified during the research. It gave the investigator a way to navigate connections across material that would otherwise remain scattered among websites, transcripts, and documents.

The associations served as a research structure. A business-record connection, a reference in a recording, and a social-media association each carried their own source context. Keeping those contexts available supported the investigator's review of what each connection established.

This part of the work turned a collection of findings into something that could be examined across sources and followed into more detailed material.

## Building an interface for review

A custom browser dashboard presented the research in sections. Expandable reports let the investigator move from an overview into the material relevant to a particular part of the assignment.

Audio clips were incorporated into the dashboard and could be played directly in the browser. That connected the written research with the spoken passages behind it and gave the investigator a practical way to listen while reviewing the corresponding information.

The dashboard brought together written reports, relationship information, and audio. Its purpose was to make a substantial body of research easier to navigate and evaluate before preparing the client-facing findings.

## Using AI as part of the research process

AI supported the collection, extraction, and organization of information. The work included scraping public online material, working with transcripts, and bringing government and business records into the research.

The software work was part of the same assignment. A useful deliverable required an interface through which the investigator could examine the findings, open individual sections, and play the relevant audio.

That combination demonstrated how AI could fit into an existing professional workflow. The investigator commissioned the research, reviewed the resulting information, and disseminated his selected findings to the client.

## Developing the consultancy

The assignment prompted me to establish a small consultancy helping private investigators, lawyers, and other professionals use AI in their workflows.

The experience connected research services with practical tool development. Public-source collection, transcript analysis, record organization, and custom review interfaces became concrete examples of the work AI could support.

The project's deliverable was research organized for a professional investigator's review. The consultancy grew from demonstrating those capabilities within an actual assignment and identifying opportunities to apply them in other professional workflows.
