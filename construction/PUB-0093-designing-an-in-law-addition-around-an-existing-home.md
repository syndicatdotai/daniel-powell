# Designing an In-Law Addition Around an Existing Home

An in-law addition to an existing Connecticut home brought together budget constraints, zoning requirements, and accessibility considerations. Engaged directly by the homeowner, I developed the concept around the intended occupants and the existing property.

The design considered how an older couple would use the space, how the addition would connect to the house, and which elements could be accommodated within the available budget. ADA principles provided an informal reference for considering accessibility during layout development.

### Coordinating the existing conditions

The age of the house and its roof-mounted solar installation shaped the design work. Electrical-service placement and wiring around the solar panels were identified as concerns requiring attention alongside the proposed alterations.

Assembly research established the construction methods described in the drawings. AI located zoning information and construction references for evaluating and documenting the selected approaches.

### Maintaining the design history

Homeowner conversations, layout decisions, and working notes were retained through an Obsidian and AI-supported documentation workflow. That history kept the drawing revisions connected to the homeowner's decisions.

Separate drawing packages were prepared for zoning and building review. A request to identify specific material assemblies was addressed through additional research and drawing detail. AI supported correspondence and distribution of the drawings to the people involved.

The resulting work connected the homeowner's requirements with the site constraints and construction information needed for the submissions.
