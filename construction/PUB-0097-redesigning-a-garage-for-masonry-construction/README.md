# Redesigning a Garage for Masonry Construction

An opportunity to purchase concrete block at a discount prompted a homeowner to reconsider a garage originally drawn in conventional wood framing. The building's shape remained the same, while its construction design was reworked around masonry.

The assignment required a full structural redesign for the new material. Existing drawings provided the intended form, and the revised package needed to explain how that form would be assembled using block.

### Using AI to find authoritative instruction

AI played a substantial role in locating reputable sources and experienced people from whom to learn the masonry methods. Targeted research located the instruction needed to develop the revised design.

Working on a specific building gave that learning a practical focus. Research questions followed the drawing decisions, with answers traced to source material and applied to the assembly details.

### Developing the revised package

The research was applied to the redesign and its documentation. AI helped organize information as the drawings developed. Structural calculations were also explored for personal understanding and curiosity.

The delivered masonry drawing set carried the material change through the building's construction design.
