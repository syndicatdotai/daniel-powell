# Preparing an Office Renovation for a Project-Lead Handoff

**Public project ID:** PUB-0090

During my time with Infinity Group, I helped prepare an office renovation and then handed the project over to a new lead. The scope included demolition, new flooring, office updates, some new framing, and furniture, fixtures, and equipment.

I coordinated with the design team, established the schedule, bought out subcontractors, and helped onboard the incoming project lead.

Part of that preparation involved understanding how the proposed work interacted with the existing building. I coordinated a sprinkler contractor site visit and tracked details such as existing security equipment affected by demolition. This connected the interior renovation scope with the building systems serving the space.

The renovation also involved electrical conduit, security equipment, and different levels of work in different areas. Some spaces called for demolition and new construction, while another area was limited to finishes. Those boundaries mattered when defining subcontractor responsibilities and organizing the schedule.

I worked alongside the designers to clarify details and arrange the subcontracted work around the project requirements. That preparation was also relevant to the transition between project leads. The incoming lead needed to become familiar with the scope and the existing conditions affecting it.

The handoff brought together scope coordination, subcontractor arrangements, and scheduling. Helping onboard the next lead was part of my responsibility, alongside the preparation of the work itself.

My contribution to this project centered on that preparation and transition: working with design, arranging subcontractors, establishing the schedule, and bringing the next lead into the work. It was an opportunity to apply field understanding and project organization at the stage when the renovation was being set up for execution.
