# Preparing a Site-Planning Exhibit With AI and QGIS

A homeowner needed a basic site plan for a proposed new house. For this particular submission, the town requested an approximate indication of structure locations and explicitly confirmed that preparation did not require a licensed surveyor.

The assignment began with that request alone. I located the original subdivision survey on the town's website and used the historical drawing as the reference for a new planning exhibit.

### Combining historical and current information

The survey dated from the 1970s, and some existing structures were absent from it. The exhibit needed to bring that older reference together with current location information and the proposed building.

Inputs included the town's request, design constraints, an estimated house location, and updated locations for existing structures. These defined what the document needed to communicate.

### Directing production through QGIS

AI operated QGIS using the supplied survey and project information. The work was directed through the proposed locations and constraints, producing the document overnight.

The sheet overlaid the historical survey with commentary, approximate dimensions, and the existing and proposed structures. It brought information from different periods into a single view for the requested review.

The delivered exhibit combined the historical survey, current structures, and proposed building locations in the format requested by the town.
