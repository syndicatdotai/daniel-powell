# Using AI Renderings to Explore Exterior Materials

A general contractor needed exterior renderings for a building designed by another architect. The architectural package supplied the building's form and arrangement. The rendering assignment explored siding, roofing, and color selections using that design as the reference.

Material treatments were developed through an AI image-generation workflow. Architectural references and finish instructions guided the images, with successive revisions bringing the results closer to the intended appearance.

### Directing the material studies

Siding placement required particular attention. Horizontal lap siding and shake siding needed to appear in their intended areas, including the gables. Each generated image was checked against the architectural reference and revised for the specified material placement.

The studies explored alternative exterior treatments and seasonal settings, including fall and winter surroundings. Those settings provided illustrative contexts for comparing the proposed finishes.

### Delivering different visual approaches

The delivered images included realistic renderings and sketch-style illustrations with a pencil, charcoal, and marker-like appearance. The work took about two hours.

The commission added material and color visualization to an existing architectural package. It provided the contractor with several ways to present and consider exterior choices while retaining the original architect's building design as the starting point.
