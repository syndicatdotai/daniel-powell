# Developing an Open-Plan Home Renovation

A homeowner referral led to a residential renovation design covering a new roof, siding, doors, a window, a kitchen, and an open floor plan supported by engineered beam assemblies. The scope also addressed incorrect framing in the original construction.

The design and drawings developed through several rounds of homeowner review over a period of months. Exterior changes and internal alterations were considered together as the homeowner refined the intended use of the house.

### Giving engineering a reliable starting point

Opening the interior required documenting the existing framing and roof construction. As-built drawings, site conditions, and information about the roof assembly were supplied to the engineer for the beam calculations.

Architectural development and engineering coordination proceeded from that shared understanding of the house. The engineer calculated the beam assemblies, while the architectural drawings incorporated the renovation layout and supporting construction information.

### Carrying decisions through revisions

Homeowner decisions were translated into revised drawings throughout the design process. AI supported project documentation, while the design and drafting work drew on direct understanding of the existing building.

The delivered drawing package brought the proposed exterior work, interior layout, and engineered beam requirements together. It documented the layout and construction changes for the homeowner's renovation.
