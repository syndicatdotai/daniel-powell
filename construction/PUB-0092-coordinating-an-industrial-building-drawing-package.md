# Coordinating an Industrial Building Drawing Package

An industrial building addition required architectural coordination between an engineered foundation, a prefabricated metal structure, and an existing building. The general contractor brought me in to develop the architectural package and resolve the requirements connecting those separate scopes.

The work covered internal layouts, shelving arrangements, electrical and HVAC locations, occupancy, fire safety, and access. Foundation and primary structural engineering were provided by the respective engineering parties. Architectural development brought their information together with the owner's operating requirements and the existing site conditions.

### Resolving the gaps between packages

Comparing the documents revealed discrepancies around the connection to the existing building and differences between the foundation and metal-building information. Door locations and circulation had to work with existing openings. Storage arrangements needed to preserve egress and fire-department access.

These questions were developed into drawing changes and specific coordination items. The connection between buildings required attention to its physical location and the assembly separating the spaces. Changes to one area carried through the affected plans, elevations, and notes.

### Researching assemblies and documenting decisions

AI supported research into regulations, listed assemblies, and documented comparable construction methods across the United States. The research informed the assembly options developed in response to the fire marshal's requests. UL assembly information was incorporated into the blueprint commentary to document the selected construction.

AI also assisted with project emails, checking drawing commentary, and preparing explanations of proposed solutions. AutoCAD remained the working environment for developing and coordinating the architectural set.

Revision summaries preserved the reason for each change alongside the drawings. Updated manufacturer information, architectural layouts, and supporting references were assembled into coordinated issues for review. The work produced a package that connected the building's structural systems with its internal use, access, and fire-safety requirements.
