# Reconfiguring a Commercial Office and Building Eight Custom Workstations

**Public project ID:** PUB-0118

## Overview

An office remodel began with subdividing existing rooms in a commercial space to create additional offices. The work extended from light architectural drafting through framing, insulation, drywall, and finished surfaces, including repairs to the existing acoustical ceiling and flooring.

Custom furniture was selected for the project to complement the new office configuration. I offered to design and build the furniture, extending the remodel into a second phase that delivered eight custom desks.

## Role

My work covered the office alterations and the development of the furniture solution. That included drafting, construction, finish work, AutoCAD furniture designs, material sourcing, shop fabrication, transportation, and on-site assembly.

The two phases were connected by the same practical requirement: creating usable offices within an existing commercial interior and furnishing them in a way that worked with the revised layout.

## Reconfiguring the Existing Space

The first phase divided existing rooms into additional offices. Light architectural drafting supported the layout, followed by framing and insulation to establish the new partitions.

Drywall installation, taping, sanding, priming, painting, and trim carried the partitions through to finished surfaces. Acoustical ceiling repairs and floor repairs addressed the existing finishes affected by the alterations.

The scope required carrying the work beyond the new walls themselves. Subdividing a room changes where surfaces meet and how the new construction connects to the surrounding interior. Completing the ceiling, floor, and trim work was part of delivering the office configuration as a finished space.

## Designing Furniture Around the New Offices

The furniture was designed around the revised office layout, with work surfaces and privacy panels tailored to the space. AutoCAD was used to develop the desk designs and panel arrangements, combining cabinet-grade plywood components with prefabricated countertop work surfaces. This approach paired custom-built components with ready-made surfaces, concentrating the fabrication work on the parts that needed to fit the office configuration.

Eight desks were built as a coordinated furniture package. The design included upright privacy panels that gave individual users visual separation at their workstations.

## Upholstered Privacy Panels

The privacy-panel assemblies used cabinet-grade plywood as the rigid substrate, with foam padding beneath a durable commercial fabric covering. The foam-backed fabric treatment concealed the plywood edges and surface irregularities, creating a softer, more consistent upholstered finish.

The panel construction addressed appearance, privacy, and sound dampening together. The upright panels provided visual screening, while the foam and fabric introduced a padded treatment behind the desks. Sound dampening was part of the design intent alongside the finished appearance of the furniture.

Material sourcing therefore covered several different requirements: plywood for the panel structure, prefabricated countertops for the work surfaces, foam for the padded layer, and fabric suitable for an office setting. These materials had to work as one assembly through fabrication, handling, and installation.

## Shop Prefabrication and On-Site Assembly

A panel-fabrication and assembly method was developed so the components could be prepared in the shop and assembled at the client's premises.

That sequence made the furniture a prefabricated system rather than eight separate pieces constructed entirely inside the offices. The work included preparing the panels, completing the upholstered treatment, transporting the components, and assembling the desks on site.

Planning for transportation and installation was part of developing the furniture. The components had to be practical to fabricate in the shop and then bring together in the finished commercial space.

Material sourcing, fabrication, transportation, and assembly of the eight desks took approximately one week. That timeframe applies to the furniture phase, separately from the preceding office remodel.

## Outcome

The project combined commercial interior alterations with a custom furniture package. Existing rooms were subdivided into additional offices, and eight desks were designed and built to suit the resulting configuration.

The assignment brought drafting, construction, finish work, upholstery, and shop production into one delivery process. Understanding the room alterations provided the context for the furniture design, while prefabrication connected the AutoCAD work with efficient on-site assembly.

## Tools and Methods

AutoCAD, light architectural drafting, partition framing, insulation, drywall installation and finishing, painting, trim, acoustical ceiling repair, floor repair, cabinet-grade plywood fabrication, upholstered privacy panels, prefabricated countertop work surfaces, shop prefabrication, and on-site furniture assembly.
