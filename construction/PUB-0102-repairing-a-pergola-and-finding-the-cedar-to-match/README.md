# Repairing a Pergola and Finding the Cedar to Match

Rot in an exterior cedar pergola required selective replacement while retaining the surrounding assembly. An architect engaged me to carry out the repairs at his home.

The extent of the work developed as demolition exposed the wood. Additional deterioration expanded the scope, requiring decisions about which members could remain and which needed replacement.

### Locating suitable material

The repair required rough-cut cedar and a mill able to process it to the needed sizes. AI supported an intensive supplier search, including pricing research and the calls and emails needed to locate the stock and milling service.

That effort took approximately four hours. Finding the material and arranging its processing were connected parts of preparing replacement members for the existing assembly.

### Supporting and repairing the pergola

Temporary timber bracing supported the portions that remained while damaged beams were removed. Replacement work progressed with the findings from demolition.

I carried out the repairs, with the supplier and mill providing the cedar and processing. AI also supported the project documentation as the scope developed.

The work restored the pergola, and the homeowner was happy with the result. It combined hands-on repair judgment with focused sourcing and coordination to obtain material suited to the existing woodwork.
