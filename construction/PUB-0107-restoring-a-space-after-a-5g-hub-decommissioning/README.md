# Restoring a Space After a 5G Hub Decommissioning

Restoration associated with decommissioning a 5G hub required returning walls and flooring to their original condition. A contractor serving the telecommunications provider engaged me for the building-repair scope.

The work included miscellaneous framing and drywall repairs. Extended travel, late working hours, and rapid turnaround requirements shaped how the work was organized.

### Addressing the existing conditions

Additional scope became apparent as the restoration progressed. Those findings were worked into the repair effort alongside the original assignment.

The work focused on the construction and surfaces affected by the decommissioning. Site conditions determined where opening, repair, and finishing were needed.

AI supported project documentation. The assignment delivered the wall and floor restoration within the broader decommissioning project, combining practical repair work with the travel and working hours needed for a rapid turnaround.
