# Coordinating a Field Assembly Change With the Architect of Record

Construction of a new telecommunications room required heavy-gauge framing, insulation, fire-rated plywood and drywall, priming, painting, doors, and hardware. A contractor serving the provider engaged me for that scope.

The architectural drawings had been prepared without a site visit. Reviewing the location identified an assembly approach better suited to the existing conditions. The customer approved the change, which was carried into the field work.

### Documenting the installed assembly

The building department agreed with the assessment and required updated documents from the architect of record. Detailed as-built drawings of the work were prepared and supplied to the architect.

Those drawings connected the field installation with the architectural documentation. They gave the architect a basis for recording the change and described what had actually been built.

### Combining construction and documentation

The assignment extended through finishing, doors, and hardware. AI supported documentation.

The room was brought into service for the telecommunications provider. The project combined an on-site assembly decision with the detailed record needed to communicate that decision back to the architect of record.
