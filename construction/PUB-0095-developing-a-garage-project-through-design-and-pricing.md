# Developing a Garage Project Through Design and Pricing

A homeowner engaged me to develop blueprints and a construction and installation package for a garage. The proposal moved through three main design phases as pricing clarified the relationship between building size and construction method.

The first approach was a large prefabricated metal building. A smaller conventionally constructed garage close to the existing house was then considered, but its price was roughly comparable to the larger metal building. The selected direction was a smaller version of the original prefabricated approach.

### Coordinating the site and foundation

Wetlands and an existing well constrained the site. An informal site plan brought those conditions together with the homeowner's space requirements and intended use.

Foundation coordination combined observations about the property with the engineer's requirements for the selected metal structure. Excavation and concrete pricing were obtained alongside the building information so the homeowner could evaluate the proposal as a construction package.

Review of the engineered documents identified items needing updates for Connecticut installation requirements associated with a shallow frost-protected slab. Those discrepancies were brought into the engineering coordination. Structural design remained with the engineer, while site information and contractor pricing were coordinated around the developing proposal.

### Keeping bids aligned with the design

AI supported project research, site-condition documentation, emails, and sending the relevant drawings out for pricing. Each bid package used the drawings relevant to the current building and foundation proposal.

Successive pricing rounds informed the building selection and established the scope for excavation, concrete, and installation.
