# Reviewing and Updating AutoCAD Drawing Notes Remotely

## LinkedIn Summary

I'm using AI to review the written notes on my AutoCAD drawings and make changes remotely through Codex.

I can have it go through the commentary, check for inconsistent wording, flag missing information, and help clarify descriptions. That includes checking whether notes in different parts of the drawing set are saying the same thing consistently.

I can also research materials and product selections as part of that process. Once I've decided what I want to use, I can have the relevant product descriptions and manufacturer information brought directly into the AutoCAD document.

Sometimes that means carrying over the manufacturer's wording. Other times it means working that information into a drawing note that clearly describes the selection and what it applies to.

I can direct those edits while I'm away from my drafting computer. If a material selection changes or a note needs more detail, I can work through the information with Codex and have the drawing updated remotely.

## Overview

I use AI to review the text on my AutoCAD drawings and make edits remotely through Codex. This includes checking existing notes, researching materials and product selections, and bringing the relevant manufacturer information into the drawing documents.

The written information is a substantial part of the drawing set. Notes explain selections, describe requirements, and give context to what is shown. I can work on that information remotely along with the edits to the AutoCAD file.

## Role

I direct the review and decide which materials, products, and descriptions belong in the drawings. Codex helps me examine the existing text, work through supporting information, and carry out the edits I request.

## Scope Of Work

The workflow covers drawing notes, annotations, material descriptions, and product information. I can use it to review wording already in the set or develop new notes as selections are made.

A review can look for unclear descriptions, missing information, or inconsistent wording across the document. Where the same selection is described in several places, I can have those references checked for consistency.

## Key Actions

- Review existing drawing commentary for clarity and consistency.
- Identify notes that need additional information or more precise wording.
- Research materials and products being considered for the work.
- Bring relevant manufacturer descriptions and supporting information into the drawing.
- Refine that information into notes appropriate to the selected material or product.
- Direct the resulting AutoCAD edits through the remote connection.

## Important Features

The workflow connects the research to the drawing update. I can work through a product selection and then have the relevant information placed into the AutoCAD document automatically under my direction.

Some information can use the manufacturer's original wording. Other information needs to be organized into a drawing note that explains the selection in context. The purpose is to put the applicable information where it belongs in the set, rather than simply collecting product descriptions separately.

Remote access also lets me continue this work when I am away from the drafting computer. I can explain the revision, work through the wording, and have Codex make the requested change in AutoCAD.

## Constraints And Decisions

I decide what is being selected and which information applies to the drawings. Manufacturer statements need to remain tied to the product and conditions they describe when they are incorporated into a note.

The scope described here is text review, product research, and drawing documentation. It is not a claim of automatic engineering certification or code approval.

## Deliverables

- Revised AutoCAD notes with clearer and more consistent wording.
- Material and product descriptions incorporated into the drawing documents.
- Manufacturer information included where it supports the selected product or material.
- Remote updates to the drawing file as instructions and selections change.

## Outcome

I can handle the research, written commentary, and AutoCAD updates remotely. A change to a selection or a request for more detail can be worked through with Codex and carried into the drawing without waiting until I am back at the drafting computer.

## Tools And Methods

AutoCAD provides the drawing environment. Codex supports remote interaction, text review, research, and execution of the requested edits. Product descriptions and manufacturer documentation provide source material for the notes.

This is a directed workflow: I explain what needs to be reviewed or changed, work through the information, and specify what should be included in the drawing.

## Evidence And Limitations

This record describes my use of AI for drawing commentary, material research, and remote AutoCAD edits. It does not include private project details or claim a measured time saving, a specific construction outcome, or independent verification of every manufacturer statement.
