# From Field Voice Notes to Annual Wildlife Reports

## LinkedIn Summary

I'm using AI to prepare my annual nuisance wildlife control operator reports for Connecticut's Department of Energy and Environmental Protection, or DEEP.

The record starts with voice notes from the job. I dictate where I was, what traps I placed, what bait I used, which species I handled, and what happened. That includes exclusion or eviction methods, lethal control methods when used, and relocation details when applicable.

I also take photos and upload them with context. AI organizes the information into Markdown job records in my Obsidian vault, with the photos kept alongside the notes.

At the end of the reporting year, my automation pulls that information together, downloads the appropriate PDF from the state website, and fills out the report in the required format.

I review the completed forms and approve them before anything is sent to DEEP. The job details are already documented throughout the year, so the reporting starts from those records.

## Overview

I use AI to organize my nuisance wildlife control job records and prepare my annual activity reports for the Connecticut Department of Energy and Environmental Protection (DEEP).

The process begins with the information I capture during the year. Voice notes, photographs, and job updates become the source records used to prepare the reporting forms.

## Role

I document the field work, provide the details of what occurred, and review the completed reports. The automation handles organizing the information and preparing the forms. Submission follows my review and approval.

## Scope Of Work

My job records include the location, traps placed, bait used, species handled, and control methods. I record exclusion or eviction work, lethal control methods when used, and relocation details where applicable.

These operational notes contain more context than the annual form alone. Trap placement, bait, and photos remain useful parts of the job record even when they do not have a dedicated field on the reporting form.

## Key Actions

- Dictate field updates describing the job and the work performed.
- Photograph site conditions and upload the images with explanatory context.
- Organize the notes into Markdown job records in Obsidian.
- Keep species, control methods, and handling outcomes connected to the relevant job.
- Compile the recorded information for the annual report.
- Download the appropriate state PDF and populate the relevant fields.
- Review and approve the completed forms before submission.

## Important Features

The same information supports both day-to-day job documentation and annual reporting. I can describe the work in ordinary speech, then have AI organize it into records that can be used again when the report is prepared.

Photos remain associated with the job notes. They provide supporting context for the work without requiring the entire photo collection to become part of the state submission.

The reporting automation takes the recorded information and places it into the appropriate form layout. It prepares a completed document for me to review rather than treating generation as approval to send it.

## Constraints And Decisions

The report needs to reflect what actually happened on the job. The recorded species, methods, and outcomes are the basis for the entries, and I review the populated forms before approving submission.

Field documentation and state reporting have different levels of detail. The job record retains the broader context; the report uses the information applicable to its fields.

## Deliverables

- Markdown job records containing field notes and wildlife handling information.
- Photographs stored with the relevant job context.
- Populated annual reporting forms prepared from those records.
- A completed report available for review and approval before submission to DEEP.

## Outcome

My annual reporting begins with information I have documented throughout the year. The automation brings those records together and handles the form preparation, while I review the finished report before it is sent.

## Tools And Methods

Voice dictation, AI assisted organization, photographs, Obsidian Markdown records, and automated PDF form preparation using the appropriate government forms.

Connecticut DEEP provides the official [NWCO reporting information and forms](https://portal.ct.gov/DEEP/Wildlife/Nuisance-Wildlife/Information-for-Licensed-NWCOs). Its [activity report form](https://portal.ct.gov/-/media/DEEP/wildlife/pdf_files/nwco/NWCOactivityreportpdf.pdf) includes control methods and handling outcomes.

## Evidence And Limitations

This describes my documentation and reporting workflow. It does not claim that a particular annual report has already been submitted or accepted by DEEP. The automation prepares the paperwork; my review and approval come before submission.
