# Using Codex to Prepare a Building Review Response

## LinkedIn Summary

I recently submitted blueprints to a town for review for a new addition. After a few weeks, they sent an RFI, a request for information.

The request is not overly complex, but it can be tedious. They want further details about building assembly methods, products, window types, model numbers, etc.

This is where I use Codex to help me find the appropriate materials and assembly methods. I use AI to help me find windows and doors with the correct sizing and energy ratings that match my design intent.

Feeding the document I received from the town to AI allows me to systematically answer their questions. I can send these agents out to find the appropriate materials and grab the details from the appropriate websites, including model numbers, construction methods, installation requirements, and energy ratings.

All that information can be placed into a response to the town.

Additionally, using computer control with Codex, I can have AI input information in the correct forms and locations on the AutoCAD drawings. AI is not directly doing line work or creating structures. It is simply inputting information.

I can then craft a document using the workflows and skills I already have set up, including the building official’s question and my response.

Due to the nature of this workflow, I could have almost all of it done from my phone, allowing Codex to use my computer at the office while I interact with it remotely. I can dictate the commentary I want on the documentation. On the back end, I’m using my HTML, CSS, and JavaScript framework to publish to a PDF.

I send these voice prompts to Codex and check on the progress in 45 minutes or an hour. It allows me to have Codex working in the background while I am doing other tasks.

It’s typically finding things and waiting for my approval. Everything that goes out the door as a finished product is directly approved by me.

## Overview

A request for information followed the submission of drawings for a residential addition. The questions concerned assembly methods, product selections, and supporting technical information. Episode 6 of Reasoning on Site describes how Daniel uses Codex to help research those details and prepare response documentation.

The starting point is the building official's request. Giving that document to the AI provides a set of questions to work through, including the information needed for window and door selections.

## Role

Daniel submitted the drawings and describes directing the research, defining the design intent, dictating response commentary, and reviewing finished material. Product research and document preparation can be delegated to Codex; Daniel retains approval of anything issued as a finished product.

## Scope Of Work

The workflow covers material and assembly research, collection of product information, preparation of answers to review questions, and entry of supporting information on existing drawings. It also includes producing a separate response document through Daniel's existing PDF workflow.

For the addition discussed, Daniel describes standard materials as sufficient and the design constraints as limited. The research therefore focuses on finding appropriate selections and the details needed to explain them.

## Key Actions

1. Provide the review request to Codex so the research follows the questions actually asked.
2. Research candidate materials, windows, and doors against the design intent and relevant selection requirements.
3. Gather model numbers, construction methods, installation requirements, and applicable energy ratings from product websites.
4. Prepare response commentary that pairs the building official's question with Daniel's answer.
5. Use computer control, when directed, to enter supporting information in the appropriate locations on existing AutoCAD drawings.
6. Generate the response PDF through the existing HTML, CSS, and JavaScript framework, with Daniel reviewing finished material before it is issued.

These steps describe the workflow discussed in the episode, including capabilities Daniel says he can use. They do not establish that every step was completed for this particular response.

## Important Features

The same review request guides both the research and the written response. Product details can be collected alongside the question they address instead of remaining separate from the explanation.

Daniel describes voice prompts and periodic progress checks as a way to direct background work while handling other tasks. He also describes the possibility of managing almost the entire workflow from a phone while Codex operates the office computer remotely.
